If a reader written from the spec alone works, the spec is complete. Every place it breaks is logged as a gap: a measurable result, not a guess.

The agents follow clean-room rules, the same method used to legally reimplement software. The Analyst can only look at bytes: no web, no documentation, not even what the model might remember about the format. Every claim needs an offset and evidence. The important one is the Builder: it only sees the spec. If its reader works on every file, the spec really captured the format. Every time the spec was silent or wrong, the Builder logged a gap, and those gaps are one of my results.
