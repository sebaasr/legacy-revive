Legacy Revive

Agents that recover abandoned software from its files alone, then carry it forward into maintainable web apps

Sebastian Rodriguez · New College of Florida · Fall 2026

Hi, I’m Sebastian. My project is Legacy Revive: a team of AI agents that takes old, abandoned software, figures out how it works from its files alone, with no documentation and no source code, and then rebuilds it as a modern web app you can open in any browser.
