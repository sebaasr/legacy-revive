The clean room isn’t just a rule, it’s a test. Readers written from the spec alone opened all 18 files and passed their tests. The Builders also logged 31 places where the spec was silent or ambiguous. And they caught one real mistake: the ZZT spec said walls have seven shapes, but the Builder counted fifteen in the actual files. The Analyst had generalized from title screens. That’s the kind of error a single agent would never catch on its own.
