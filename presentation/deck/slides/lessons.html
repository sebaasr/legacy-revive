V2

Hold-out tests for the final Verifier. Stopped the pipeline from grading its own homework.

Every prompt is logged in English in PROMPTS.md, with links to the full transcripts.

The assignment asked us to track our prompts, so I logged every one in English. Looking back, four turns shaped the project. My professor asking to modernize, not only recover, added the last two agents. Saying HyperCard was just an example is what led to the shared format and the second program. Asking to use reverse engineering made the Analyst work from bytes with a runnable probe behind every claim. And the most important orchestration decision: giving the final Verifier hold-out tests, so the system couldn’t grade its own homework.
